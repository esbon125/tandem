\chapter{Known limitations}\label{cap:limitations}

\section{Speed}

The decoder runs at 0.5 to 0.8 times real time depending on the resolution.
The limit is in the core's motion compensation stage, not in memory or the
clock (measured in the project). It suits recorded clips, not live sources
that cannot accept flow control.

\section{Conformance}

The 37 MPEG-2 conformance streams in the test bench decode identically to the
reference decoder or within the difference the standard allows between two
conforming IDCTs (mean absolute error $\leq 0.04$). Two bugs of the original
core, which caused differences in predicted pictures and with reloaded
quantiser matrices, are fixed as of bitstream \cmd{0.1.0+918dde0}.

\section{Others}

\begin{itemize}
  \item No video output: the board has no video connector; the output is the
    network.
  \item MPEG-1 is not supported.
  \item With TLS, delivery is limited to about 15 frames per second at
    704×480.
  \item Resolution changes within one clip: supported by the protocol, not
    exhaustively verified on hardware.
\end{itemize}
