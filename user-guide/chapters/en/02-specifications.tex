\chapter{Specifications}

\section{Formats}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Input} & MPEG-2 video elementary stream (ISO/IEC 13818-2). \\
\textbf{Profile} & Main Profile, 4:2:0 chroma. Frame and field pictures. \\
\textbf{Resolution} & Up to 1920\,×\,1088 by the memory map; verified on
  hardware up to 720\,×\,576. \\
\textbf{Output} & Planar YUV 4:2:0 (I420), 8 bits per sample, in display
  order. \\
\textbf{Clip length} & Unlimited (input and output are streamed). \\
\midrule
\textbf{Not supported} & MPEG-1; scalability and data partitioning; 4:2:2
  and 4:4:4; program/transport streams (the client must extract the video
  elementary stream). \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section{Performance}

Measured on the hardware with the project's regression tool
(\cmd{tools/regress}), five runs per stream after a warm-up run. The
\emph{decode rate} counts from the start of the DMA to the last frame store
write.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l c r r r@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Stream} & \textbf{Resolution} & \textbf{Frames} & \textbf{fps} & \textbf{× real time} \\
\midrule
tek60          & 704\,×\,480 &  60 & 23.7 & 0.79 \\
Tek-5-long     & 704\,×\,480 & 150 & 22.8 & 0.76 \\
mei-2-60f      & 704\,×\,480 &  61 & 21.1 & 0.70 \\
tcela-7        & 720\,×\,480 &  61 & 19.5 & 0.65 \\
sony-ct2       & 704\,×\,480 &  24 & 19.6 & 0.65 \\
nokia6-60      & 720\,×\,576 &  60 & 12.2 & 0.49 \\
hhi-burst-long & 720\,×\,576 &  46 & 12.9 & 0.52 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\paragraph{End to end} From the PC, with the client library and the
\cmd{mpeg2fpgad} service without TLS, tek60 is received complete at
\textbf{20\,fps} and Tek-5-long at 19.2\,fps: about 85\,\% of what the decoder
alone delivers.

\begin{nota}[Note]
The decoder is slower than real time (0.5 to 0.8×). For recorded clips this
is not a problem: flow control makes the client receive everything, only more
slowly. A live source that cannot be paused, on the other hand, would overflow
any buffer.
\end{nota}

\section{TLS}

TLS is optional because encryption runs on the board's processors, which have
no cryptographic extensions. Measured end to end on the board:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}l r r@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Mode} & \textbf{MB/s} & \textbf{704×480 frames/s} \\
\midrule
Plain HTTP                 & 29.0 & $\sim$57 \\
TLS 1.2 ChaCha20-Poly1305  &  7.6 & $\sim$15 \\
TLS 1.2 AES-128-GCM        &  4.5 & $\sim$9 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

With TLS, delivery is limited to about 15 frames per second at 704×480. The
service only offers ChaCha20, the fastest on this CPU.

\section{Conformance}

The decoder is verified against the ISO conformance streams and the reference
decoder (\cmd{tools/mpeg2dec}). Each release's detailed result is in its
notes; known differences are described in chapter~\ref{cap:limitations}.
