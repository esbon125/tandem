\chapter{Operation and troubleshooting}

\section{Service status}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=shell]
ssh root@board systemctl status mpeg2fpgad
ssh root@board journalctl -u mpeg2fpgad -f
\end{lstlisting}

The log records every request and, for each decode, how many frames were sent,
in how long and whether it was complete.

\section{The summary of each decode}

The END record carries what is needed to tell how a decode went:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l X@{}}
\toprule
\cmd{complete} & one frame was delivered for every frame in the clip \\
\cmd{decoder.error} & the decoder met something it could not parse; frames may have errors, the device stays healthy \\
\cmd{decoder.watchdog} & the decoder stopped; a \cmd{reset} is advisable \\
\cmd{capture\_lag\_max} & how many frames waited to be read; 0 or 1 is normal, 2 or more means overload \\
\cmd{timing} & time per stage on the board: reading, sending, waiting for the DMA, time with the decoder frozen \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section{Common problems}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright}p{42mm} X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symptom} & \textbf{What to check} \\
\midrule
\cmd{/v1/health} does not answer & network and address; \cmd{systemctl status mpeg2fpgad} \\
401 with the right token & the token changed (rotation); copy it again \\
Persistent 409 & another client is decoding; \cmd{/v1/status} shows it \\
503 & driver not loaded or overlay not applied: \cmd{systemctl status mpeg2fpga-overlay} \\
\cmd{complete: false} without error & the clip ends with an incomplete picture, or has pictures the core does not support (chapter~\ref{cap:limitations}) \\
Frames slower than expected & TLS on; a client reading slowly (high \cmd{timing.frozen\_s}) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\section{Recovery}

In normal operation clips are chained without resetting anything. If a decode
ends with \cmd{watchdog}, or \cmd{/v1/status} reports \cmd{error}:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python]
dev.reset()
\end{lstlisting}
